# Content research — 2026-10-26

A bounded investigation of named residual weapon and Genesis-power candidates found no demonstrated ready-to-enable additional player weapon or power. This is a result for the inspected candidates, not proof that the entire game contains no unused content. No speculative restoration controls were added.

| Candidate | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Bruno's scalpel, Sister of Mercy's whip, episode props | Plain Item assets, without an authored player equipment/attack contract. A prop or charm mesh does not establish a usable weapon. |
| Old Genesis Configuration | Legacy melee-item representation with zero attack montages; no demonstrated additional combat form. |
| Cultist's Genesis pistol | Enemy-only actor contract, incompatible with player equipment; no authored pickup bridge was found for the inspected actor. |
| Existing melee weapons and Genesis variants | Already covered by the eligible item registry/give path; no evidence of restored cut content. |
| Hellshot, Hellvision and Hellgun names | Legacy presentation/configuration hooks and effects do not establish an independent playable grantable power. Captured cheat-event names did not supply an implementation. |
| Telekinesis and other ability variants | Existing interaction/helper/damage-handler abilities, rather than demonstrated additional unlockable player powers. |

The investigation compared full package/class identities for 188 Item-derived records, directory metadata from nine installed content containers, and targeted reflected/native ability and equipment evidence. Basenames and folder names were not treated as proof of cut status. Full proprietary payload dependency graphs and every story encounter were outside the scope.

Creating a player-compatible scalpel, whip or enemy pistol would require new equipment, animations, attack behavior and runtime acceptance. That is future gameplay adaptation, not a verified dormant feature that can simply be enabled. Paid/bonus-item exclusions and the existing item/power catalog are unchanged. Private captures, raw dumps, offsets, local paths and game assets are not distributed.
